FTSO signal provider whose address names the file.

## Why they are here

Each file was uploaded by the provider it depicts, through flareregistry.com,
for the purpose of identifying that provider in the published list and in
applications that read it. They are committed here so GitHub's raw CDN can
serve them, which is the same git-backed hosting model the standard provider
list uses.

Serving a provider's logo alongside its entry, in a list or interface that
identifies providers, is the use they were supplied for. Any other use is a
matter between you and the provider that owns the mark.

## If one of these is yours

To change or withdraw a logo, sign in at https://flareregistry.com with the
address that owns the listing and replace it, or contact Burst Labs at
https://www.burstlabs.io and it will be removed.
